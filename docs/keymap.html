How layers are reached

  • NAV: hold the left bottom-row thumb key (outer).
  • NUM&SYM: hold the right bottom-row thumb key (inner).
  • ADJUST: hold NAV and NUM together. Bluetooth profiles, USB/BT toggle, bootloader and reset live here.
  • FN: hold the right middle-row thumb key (inner); tap gives AltGr. Bluetooth profile select and BT clear are on the FN layer's top row, right hand: inner = profile 0, pinky = clear.
  • NUMPAD: hold the left middle-row thumb key (inner); tap gives Space. The pinky top-row key on NUMPAD toggles it on permanently.
  • COLEMAK-DH and QWERTY: toggled from the FN layer, left extra row (middle = Colemak, pinky = QWERTY). Toggle again to return to Gallium.
  • ONE-HAND: toggled from the NAV layer (left middle finger, top row) and from its own bottom-left thumb key.
  • Home-row mods: hold the home-row letter for the modifier shown in blue. Balanced flavour, 280 ms tapping term, 150 ms prior-idle so fast rolls stay letters.
  • Caps Word: the single left thumb key. Right single thumb key is Delete. Backspace on the left bottom thumb becomes Delete with Shift.

Bluetooth recovery

  • If Windows or the phone says "couldn't connect" after you removed the keyboard on that side, the left half still holds the old bond. Hold FN and press the right pinky top-row key (BT clear), then pair again from the host.
  • The right half never types over USB; it only charges there. Its keys reach the host through the left half over Bluetooth.
  • If the halves stop seeing each other after a settings reset, flash settings_reset to both halves, then the left and right firmware, and power both on together.
